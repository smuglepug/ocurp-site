Reduce a width and height to their lowest-term ratio by greatest common divisor, list the standard resolutions that share it, and solve the scale needed to fit a source size inside a given box.

GCD REDUCTION RESOLUTION MATCHES SCALE TO FIT

Enter a pixel size and it is reduced by its greatest common divisor, so 1920×1080 becomes 16:9 because the gcd is 120 — not by dividing by hand until something looks tidy. The matches list compares the reduced pair, which is why 854×480 does not appear under 16:9: it reduces to 427:240.
