Regular Polygon Calculator

Interior and exterior angles, the apothem, circumradius, perimeter and area of a regular n-gon from the side or the circumradius, with a drawn polygon at any vertex count.

Math & convertPolygonApothemGeometryInterior Angle
Open now All tools

A side count and a side length are all a regular polygon needs. This splits the shape into n congruent isosceles triangles about its centre and works from the half central angle pi/n, which is where the perimeter, the interior and exterior angles, the apothem, the area, the circumradius and the inradius all come from — the apothem and the inradius being the same length twice over. The polygon is drawn to scale with its apothem marked, and the page says plainly whether your n is even or odd, because that decides whether opposite vertices can be paired. A side count below 3 or a zero side length is refused with the reason rather than dividing by zero.
