Heatmap Generator

CREATIVE — CLIENT-SIDE

Turns a grid of numbers into a colour-mapped canvas and reports the min, max, mean, standard deviation and hottest cell of the matrix.

Rows of unequal length are padded with 0 so the matrix stays rectangular.

Heatmap

How it works: each cell is normalised against the matrix minimum and maximum, then mapped through a three-stop ramp into an RGB fill; the statistics below come from the same parse the canvas was drawn from.
