Spring Resonance Calculator

Natural frequency, period, static deflection, quality factor and the damped and resonance frequencies for any mass-spring-damper, with the damping regimes named.

ScienceSpringResonanceDampingFrequency
Open now All tools

A mass on a spring with a damper in it. It gives the undamped natural frequency in both rad/s and Hz, the damping ratio against the critical value 2*sqrt(k*m), the quality factor, and the peak response frequency — and it names which of the three damping branches you are on. That last part is the whole point: at or above critical damping the system never oscillates, so there is no damped frequency and none is printed. Peak response is treated the same way, because the resonance peak disappears once zeta passes 1/sqrt(2) and a damped frequency is not the same thing as a resonance.
