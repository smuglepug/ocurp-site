Buoyancy Calculator

science — CLIENT-SIDE

Archimedes, written out: buoyant force from the fluid density, gravity and the displaced volume, then the apparent weight left over and a straight float-or-sink verdict against the object density. Pick a fluid or type a density, and keep the displaced volume separate from the object volume so a partly submerged floating body is not silently over-lifted.

Read-out

  

    Archimedes' principle: a body in a fluid is pushed up by the weight of the fluid
    it displaces, so F_b = rho_fluid g V_displaced. The verdict compares the object
    density with the fluid density, and the submerged fraction is the ratio of the two
    capped at 1. The two volumes are separate inputs on purpose. A floating object
    displaces less than its own volume, so entering the full object volume for a
    floating body overstates the lift and can push an object that should float past
    neutral. Ignored here: surface tension, which dominates for millimetre-scale
    objects and water striders.
