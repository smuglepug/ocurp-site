Noise Generator

Generate white, pink, brown or blue noise with a seed, play it, and download the WAV.

White, pink and brown noise synthesised from a seeded 32-bit generator, so the same seed always gives the same samples. RMS, peak, crest factor and per-octave energy are measured from the buffer and the result can be exported as a real WAV.

Octave balance (energy per octave band)

White noise should be flat across bands. Pink noise falls about 3 dB per octave doubling, brown about 6 dB. The measured ratios come from a real DFT of the generated buffer, not from the formula.
