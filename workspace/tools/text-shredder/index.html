Text Shredder

Shred a line of text into interleaved, sliced, repeated or random fragments, and invert it again.

Writing & textShredderTextObfuscate
Open now All tools

Cut text into strips, chunks or single characters with a seeded PRNG, then recover it from the kept positions to prove the shred is reversible. 3 MODESSEEDEDRECOVERABLECLIENT-SIDE

How it works: positions are chosen by a seeded PRNG, so a shred is reproducible from its seed. Recover takes the same shred and puts the kept characters back into their original slots, which proves nothing was lost — the panel reports whether the recovered text equals the input.
