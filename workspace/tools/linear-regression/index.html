Linear Regression (OLS)

mathematics — CLIENT-SIDE

Ordinary least squares, worked by hand rather than handed to a library. Type the observations and the page builds the normal equations from the five sums that matter, then reports the slope, intercept, r, R-squared, adjusted R-squared and the standard error of estimate — and gives the t and F statistics an honest verdict against the 5% critical value for your degrees of freedom. A perfect line is reported as a perfect line: the residual variance is exactly zero, so the t statistic is undefined and the page says so instead of printing a zero.
