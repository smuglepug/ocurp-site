FIFO Queue Simulator

Step a queue through producer, consumer and overflow events and watch the depth and drop count move.

Data & codeQueueFifoSimulation

A discrete-event first-in-first-out queue with a slot limit and three overflow policies: reject the newcomer, evict the oldest, or block the producer. Reports depth, throughput, head-of-line wait in ticks and how long the backlog takes to drain.

Discrete-event simulation: the clock advances in equal ticks and each tick the producer tries to enqueue and the consumer tries to dequeue. Head-of-line wait time is measured in ticks, not rendered as an animation.
