Color Mixer

design — CLIENT-SIDE

Blend two colours in linear-light RGB, sRGB or HSL and compare all three at once, with luma, contrast ratios and an 11-step ramp. 3 BLEND SPACESWCAG CONTRAST11-STEP RAMPCLIENT-SIDE

How it works: each channel is linearised, averaged with weight t, then re-encoded. Mixing #FF5F1F with #050505 50/50 gives a visibly brighter result in linear light (about #B2734F) than in sRGB (about #7A2F0F) — that gap is the whole point of the tool.
