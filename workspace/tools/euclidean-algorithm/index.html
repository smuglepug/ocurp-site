Euclidean Algorithm

MATHEMATICS — CLIENT-SIDE

The greatest common divisor of any list of integers, computed the way a person computes it: repeated subtraction, one row per step. Bezout coefficients are folded across the whole list and multiplied back out so you can watch them sum to the gcd exactly, alongside the lcm and the modular inverse of any number you name.

Input

The chain is the real subtraction algorithm, not a modulo summary, so every row is one subtraction you can check. Bezout coefficients multiply the whole list back to the gcd exactly.
