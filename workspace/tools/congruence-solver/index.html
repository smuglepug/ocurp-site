Congruence Solver

mathematics — CLIENT-SIDE

A Chinese Remainder Theorem solver that shows its working and refuses to lie. Congruences go in one per line, the answer comes out as one class x modulo a period, and every intermediate period is the running least common multiple. When the constraints contradict each other it names the pair that clashes and why, instead of returning a number that satisfies nothing.

CHINESE REMAINDER PERIOD = LCM BRUTE-FORCE CHECK

One line per congruence, x = r (mod m): 2 mod 3, 3 mod 5. The solver adds them one at a time, widening the period each time, and stops with a plain reason when two of them contradict each other - which is the whole difficulty of the problem and the thing a naive tool hides.
