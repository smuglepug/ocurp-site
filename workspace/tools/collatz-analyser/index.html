mathematics — CLIENT-SIDE

Walk the 3n+1 sequence and count what actually happened: steps to reach one, the largest value on the way, how many of those steps were the tripling kind, and the exact step at which a value you name is first passed through. Nothing is assumed to terminate - the walk has a step budget and says so out loud if the budget runs out first.

3N + 1 STOPPING TIME FIRST HIT

An odd value triples and adds one; an even value halves. This page walks that sequence for real rather than assuming it terminates, counting the steps, the largest value it ever reaches, how many of those steps were the tripling kind, and - for a target you name - the exact step at which the sequence first passes through it. A start value that never reaches your target reports that honestly instead of hanging.
