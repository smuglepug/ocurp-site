GCD & LCM Visualiser

A grid of numbers shaded by their real greatest common divisor with a chosen base, with the gcd and lcm of the whole run and a legend of the divisors present.

Math & convertGcdLcmVisualiser
Open now All tools

A grid of numbers shaded by their real greatest common divisor with a base you choose: the shade rises with the divisor, so multiples of the base stand out darkest. The gcd and lcm of the whole run are computed alongside it, and the legend lists the divisors actually present.

Input

Each cell is shaded from the single accent hue by its own computed gcd with the base: shade rises with the divisor, so the multiples of the base itself read darkest. Every highlight in the grid is arithmetic, not a drawn pattern.
