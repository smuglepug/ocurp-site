Image Dominant Colors

Find the colours that actually dominate a picture by re-clustering it until the palette is clean.

Clusters an image's pixels with k-means in RGB space and ranks the colours by population, with the share of the frame each one covers.

FileReader + createImageBitmap feed a canvas; pixels are clustered with k-means in RGB space. No upload.

Dominant colours

How it works: k-means on the RGB histogram, seeded with the k brightest-to-darkest spread of distinct colours and refined for twelve passes; clusters that lose all their pixels are dropped and the rest are ranked by population.
