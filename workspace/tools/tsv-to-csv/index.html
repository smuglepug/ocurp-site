TSV to CSV

Data — CLIENT-SIDE

Converts tab-separated text to any delimiter, parsing it with a state machine so quoted cells keep their tabs and embedded newlines survive intact. Ragged rows are padded to a rectangle and counted, not silently cut.

Parsed with a state machine, not a split: quoted fields keep their delimiters, doubled quotes unescape to one, and blank lines and a UTF-8 BOM are dropped. Ragged rows are padded and counted rather than silently truncated.
