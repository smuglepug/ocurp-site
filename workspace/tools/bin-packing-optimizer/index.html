Bin Packing Optimizer

algorithms — CLIENT-SIDE

Put items into the fewest bins of a fixed capacity. First-fit-decreasing is what people actually do at 3am when a bin is half full and will not close; this page runs it, then runs a proven search for the true optimum, then shows the total-size lower bound so all three answers can be compared by eye. The gap between greedy and optimal is the point.

FIRST FIT DECREASING BRANCH AND BOUND PROVEN OPTIMUM

Greedy packing opens a new bin the moment an item does not fit the current one. That is fast and it is wrong: it never looks back. The branch-and-bound search here tries every legal placement but abandons a branch the moment it has already opened as many bins as the best answer found so far, so the optimum is proven rather than guessed. The lower bound is plain arithmetic - the total size divided by the capacity, rounded up - and no packing can beat it.
