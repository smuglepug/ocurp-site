Cholesky Decomposition

Factor a symmetric positive-definite matrix into L times L-transpose with no libraries, one pivot at a time, and a clear verdict instead of a square root of a negative number when the matrix is not positive definite.

Math & convertCholeskyMatrixDecompositionSpd
Open now All tools

Factor a symmetric matrix into L L^T one pivot at a time, with no linear algebra library anywhere in the file. Every entry of L comes from a sum, a square root and a division you can read in the working pane, and the result is multiplied back out to show how far it lands from A. Give it a matrix that is not positive definite and it stops at the offending pivot and says why, rather than handing back the square root of a negative number.

A = L L^T NO LIBRARIES VERIFIED MULTIPLY-BACK

A square matrix, one row per line, entries separated by spaces or commas. It has to be symmetric — L L^T always is — so an asymmetric input is reported rather than silently symmetrised. On a non-positive pivot the page stops and names the step: taking the square root of a negative number is not a decomposition, it is a NaN.
