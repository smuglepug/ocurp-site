Decibel Calculator

Sound pressure level, intensity addition of independent sources, and inverse-distance falloff, with the arithmetic shown.

Audio & speechDecibelSoundAcousticsSpl
Open now All tools

Convert a sound pressure to SPL against the 20 microPascal threshold of hearing, add independent sources by intensity rather than by adding decibels, and propagate a level with the inverse-distance law. Click a reference level to load it. Every line of the read-out shows the arithmetic so you can redo it on paper.

Reference levels - click one to load it
Threshold of hearing p0 = 20 µPa = 20×10-6 Pa in air at 20 °C. Levels are referenced to that pressure, not to 1 µPa, and the 20log form is the pressure-ratio definition; the 10log form is the matching intensity definition.
