Full report
What each fit means

Arithmetic

Every gap is the same. Next term is the last one plus the common difference. A gap check is exact — there is nothing to solve, so a false positive is impossible.

Geometric

Every term is the previous one times a constant ratio. Fails if any term is zero (division by zero), so sequences containing 0 are not called geometric unless every term is 0.

Fibonacci-like

Fits aₙ = p·aₙ₋₁ + q·aₙ₋₂, solving for p and q from the first four terms and then checking every later term. Plain Fibonacci is the case p = q = 1. The tribonacci sequence 1, 1, 2, 4, 7, 13 is p = 1, q = 2 and is caught here too.

Polynomial

Fits aₙ = c₀ + c₁x + c₂x² + … by solving a Vandermonde system, starting at the lowest degree that reproduces every term exactly. Because n points always fit a degree n−1 polynomial, this is the weakest of the exact fits and is labelled as such.
