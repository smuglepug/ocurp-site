Resonance Frequency

LC, RC, pendulum, mass-spring and open versus closed air columns in one page, with the harmonic number.

Five resonators on one page: an LC tank, an RC time constant, a simple pendulum, a mass on a spring, and an air column with either one open end or two. Pick the harmonic number for the air column and both end conditions are shown side by side, using v = 343 m/s unless you change it. Every result prints the substitution.
