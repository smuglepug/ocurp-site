Egyptian Fraction Solver

Decompose any fraction into a sum of distinct unit fractions - the greedy Sylvester chain in exact BigInt arithmetic, plus a bounded search for the shortest expansion.

Math & convertFractionsSylvesterGreedyBigint
Open now All tools

Every proper fraction can be written as a sum of distinct unit fractions, and the proof is two lines long: subtract the largest unit fraction that does not exceed the number, and repeat. This page runs that greedy chain in exact arbitrary-precision integers, then searches a bounded space for a shorter expansion and proves the shorter one is genuinely shorter.

EXACT BIGINT GREEDY SYLVESTER CHAIN BOUNDED MINIMUM SEARCH

A fraction p/q, or a decimal like 0.4375. Denominators above 2^53 are handled as BigInt, so a greedy chain that ends near 10^17 is reported exactly rather than rounded into a lie.
