nPr and nCr for any n and r, computed as exact integers through the whole quotient chain rather than a float ratio, with a factorial table for checking and a listing mode that writes out the arrangements themselves when r is small enough to read.

Input

nPr and nCr are computed as integer products of the whole quotient chain, never as a float ratio, so 5000 choose 3 comes back as one exact integer rather than a rounded one.
