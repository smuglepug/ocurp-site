Luhn Validator

The Luhn checksum used by card numbers, IMEIs and NRIC numbers, worked digit by digit with the total and its remainder shown - and stated as a format check, not an authorisation.

The Luhn checksum that payment card numbers, IMEIs and Singapore NRIC numbers use, worked out digit by digit in front of you. The page doubles the right digits, subtracts 9 where the result exceeds a digit, adds it up and shows the total modulo 10 — and states plainly that a number passing this check is not a number anyone has issued you.

Verdict
The algorithm, digit by digit — doubled digits in colour

Luhn catches transpositions and single-digit typos, which is all it does. It is not a payment authorisation and not proof a card exists: every number printed here can be constructed to pass, and the page says so rather than implying the check authorises anything. A real card number also fails a Luhn algorithm, so this is a format check only.
