Color Shades & Tints

COLOR — CLIENT-SIDE

Turns one hex colour into a nine-step tint ramp, a nine-step shade ramp, its HSL model, luminance and contrast ratios.

Accepts #RGB and #RRGGBB. Anything else is rejected with a reason.

Model
Tints and shades

How it works: a tint at p mixes the colour toward white by p, a shade mixes toward black by p, each channel rounded to the nearest integer; the ramp runs in ten-point steps from 10% to 90%.
