Rewrites shouty text as a proper sentence, capitalising after every full stop, question mark and exclamation mark while leaving NASA, iPhone, eBay, 3.14 and A1 alone. The difficulty is knowing which words not to touch.

The hard part is not capitalising the first letter, it is knowing which words to leave alone. Tokens holding an interior capital, a digit or a period, and all-caps runs of two or more letters, are treated as proper nouns or codes and copied through untouched.
