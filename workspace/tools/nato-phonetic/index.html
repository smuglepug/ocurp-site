NATO Phonetic Alphabet

Spell any text out in the NATO phonetic alphabet, with a harder alphabet option.

Spell any text as ICAO phonetic words, with a toggle back to the 1943 Able / Baker form and a diff of the letters where the two disagree. NATO + ICAO 1943LETTER DIFFCLIENT-SIDE

How it works: every A–Z maps to its ICAO phonetic word, digits get the standard number words, and anything else is counted as a gap rather than silently dropped — you always see how many characters were actually covered.
