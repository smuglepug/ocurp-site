Continued Fractions

MATHEMATICS — CLIENT-SIDE

Paste a real number or a fraction and get its continued fraction expansion, the ladder of convergents that bracket it, and the tightest rational you can write under a denominator cap. Every convergent is printed against the 1 over q squared error bound it is promised to keep, and the tool says so out loud if one ever fails to.

Input

Each convergent is one better than the last on the alternating side of the number. The bound column is the promise the convergents keep: the error is always under one over q squared.
