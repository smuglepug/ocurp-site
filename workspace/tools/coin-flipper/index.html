Coin Flip Probability

A seeded coin you can reproduce, with the exact binomial probability of the outcome you got, the chance of seeing at least that many heads and a two-sided p-value.

Math & convertBinomialProbabilityP-ValueSeeded
Open now All tools

Flip a coin with a seed you control, then find out how likely that result actually was. The generator is a specified seeded PRNG, so seed 1 always gives the same run — you cannot keep clicking until the numbers please you — and the page computes the exact binomial probability of the outcome it produced, the probability of seeing at least that many heads, and a two-sided p-value. Ten heads in ten shows up as 0.098%.

Result
The run

Seeded, so the same seed gives the same run — which is the point. A coin simulator that reshuffles on every click cannot be used to reason about what a result means, because you can keep clicking until you get the answer you liked. The page runs the exact binomial probabilities for the outcome it got and reports how surprising it was, so 10 heads in 10 shows as 0.098% of fair-coin outcomes rather than looking like a result that simply happened.
