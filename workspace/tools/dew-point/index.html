Notes

This is the Magnus form with constants 17.27 and 237.7 °C. It is an empirical fit to saturation curves, not a derivation from thermodynamics, so it is an approximation: good to about ±0.4 °C over 0–50 °C and 10–100% RH, and it degrades below 0 °C (supercooled vapour, frost) and above 50 °C. Near 0% RH it diverges. For a psychrometric calculation at low temperature use a reference such as Sonntag or a full equation of state instead.
