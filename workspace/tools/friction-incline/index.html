Friction on an Incline

Critical angle from static friction, then the slide or hold verdict, acceleration down the ramp and the work done against friction.

Math & convertFrictionInclineMuMechanics
Open now All tools

A block on a slope with separate static and kinetic friction. The page computes the critical angle from tan(theta_c) = mu_s, gives the slide or hold verdict, then the sliding acceleration, the time to the bottom of the ramp and the work lost to friction - all from the same numbers, with each substitution printed.
