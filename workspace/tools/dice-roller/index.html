Dice Roller

GENERATORS — CLIENT-SIDE

Rolls d4 to d100 with modifiers and keep-highest or keep-lowest rules, all from a seeded generator you can pin to a single repeated face.

Each face comes from a seeded mulberry32, so a seed reproduces a roll exactly.

Result

How it works: a face is 1 + floor(random x sides); keeping only the highest or lowest is applied after the roll, and the modifier lands on the total rather than on each die.
