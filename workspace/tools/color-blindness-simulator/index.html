Colour Blindness Simulator

Simulation uses the Machado, Oliveira & Fernandes (2009) severity-1.0 model for dichromats, blended toward the original image by the strength slider. Roughly 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women have some form of colour vision deficiency.
