Metronome

Music — CLIENT-SIDE

A click that keeps time: BPM, bar length and subdivision from eighth notes to sixteenths, with a lookahead scheduler so the tempo never drifts with the frame rate. 120 BPM is a beat every 500 ms exactly, and each eighth is 250 ms.
