Dot Product Calculator

Multiply and add vectors, or matrix times vector times matrix, with the working shown term by term and the magnitude alongside.

Math & convertVectorDot ProductMatrix
Open now All tools

Two vectors in, everything that follows from a single sum out. The dot product comes first, then both magnitudes, the cosine similarity, the angle between the vectors in both degrees and radians, and the projection of either vector onto the other — each shown with the formula that produced it. The zero vector is treated as the real case it is: the dot product is 0, and the cosine, the angle and the projection are reported as undefined with the reason, rather than handed back as NaN or Infinity as if those were answers.

DOT PRODUCT PROJECTION ANGLE AND COSINE

Two equal-length vectors, one number per line or separated by spaces and commas. Everything on the right is built from the same three sums the textbook uses — a·b, |a|² and |b|² — so the cosine, the angle and the projection can never disagree with the dot product they came from.
