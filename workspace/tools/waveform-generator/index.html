Waveform Generator

Render a sine, square, saw or triangle wave with harmonics and phase, play it, and export a WAV.

Sine, square, sawtooth, triangle and a duty-variable pulse, built by summing real harmonics sample by sample. Peak, RMS, crest factor and zero-crossing count are measured from the rendered samples, so a 220 Hz square really does cross zero 440 times a second.
