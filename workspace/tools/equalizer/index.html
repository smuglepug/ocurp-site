Five-Band Equalizer

Audio — CLIENT-SIDE

Five chained BiquadFilterNodes, two shelves and three peaking bands, driven by sliders in decibels. Each dB value is converted with 10^(dB/20) and the resulting response curve is drawn from the filter equations, not from a picture of one.

Each band is a BiquadFilterNode chained in series: two shelving filters at the ends and peaking filters in the middle. dB is converted to a linear gain with 10^(dB/20), which is the amplitude form of the decibel.
