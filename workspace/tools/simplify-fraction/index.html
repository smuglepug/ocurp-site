Simplify Fraction

Reduce a fraction to lowest terms and see the work. The page runs the Euclidean algorithm, reports the greatest common divisor that was divided out, converts to a mixed number, and prints an exact decimal when one exists — and says plainly when the denominator carries a prime factor other than 2 or 5, which is the condition for the expansion never terminating.

Why — the Euclidean algorithm, step by step

The reduction is exact: dividing by the greatest common divisor leaves an equivalent fraction, never an approximation. A decimal is printed only when the reduced denominator has no prime factor other than 2 and 5 — the case where the expansion terminates — so the page reports 0.333333 for 1/3 as a rounded value and says so, rather than implying a finite answer exists.
