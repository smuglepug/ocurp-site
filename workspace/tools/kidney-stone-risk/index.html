Kidney Stone Risk Estimate

Water intake, urine volume, urinary calcium and urinary oxalate scored 0-100 with each factor's points and the arithmetic shown, so you can see which input moved the number. An educational estimate, not medical advice.

Feed in your water intake, urine output, urinary calcium and urinary oxalate and the page shows exactly how many points each one contributes to a 0-100 score, with the arithmetic printed so you can redo it by hand. An educational estimate built on the four widely published modifiable risk factors for calcium oxalate stones - not medical advice, not a diagnosis, and not a validated risk model.

EDUCATIONAL ESTIMATE NOT MEDICAL ADVICE
Estimate
The four factors
Every number in the score
Read-out

  

    Read this as a way to see which input moved the
    number, nothing more. The four factors weighted here - low fluid intake,
    low urine volume, high urinary calcium and high urinary oxalate - are the
    standard, widely published modifiable risk factors for calcium oxalate stones,
    and the arithmetic below is a plain linear ramp built for transparency, not a
    validated risk model. A score here is not a diagnosis, not a probability, and not
    a reason to change anything about how you live. Stones are common, most pass on
    their own, and the questions worth asking a clinician are about the urine
    chemistry of a real 24-hour collection and your own history, not about a
    calculator.
