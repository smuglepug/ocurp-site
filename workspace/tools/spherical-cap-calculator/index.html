The lens of sphere left above a cutting plane: its volume, the area of the flat base left by the cut, the curved spherical area over it, both together, and how much of the whole sphere that volume is. The base circle comes from the Pythagorean theorem inside the sphere, sqrt(R^2 - (R-h)^2), and the curved area from the spherical zone formula 2*pi*R*h. A cap is by definition shallower than the radius, so h at or above R is refused with the reason — that is a hemisphere, not a cap.
