Notes

The Beer-Lambert law is A = ε·b·c: absorbance is proportional to concentration and to path length. It assumes a dilute, clear, single-species solution - stray scattering or a second absorbing species bends the curve and the straight-line ε stops meaning anything. Transmittance and absorbance are related by A = −log₁₀(%T/100): the base is 10, not e. A = 1 is 10% transmittance; with ln you get 4.3% and every dilution after that is wrong.
