Loan Amortization Schedule

finance — CLIENT-SIDE

A fully amortising loan, worked month by month. Give it a principal, a nominal annual rate and a term in months and it returns the level monthly payment, the total interest, the total paid, and a year-by-year table of interest, principal and remaining balance. The schedule is a loop you can read: interest is the opening balance times the monthly rate, principal is whatever is left of the payment, and the last payment absorbs the rounding residue so the balance closes on zero instead of a few cents. An interest-free loan is handled as the limit case it is, not as a division by zero.
