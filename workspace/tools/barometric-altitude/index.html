Barometric Altitude & Hypsometric Pressure

science — CLIENT-SIDE

Two pressure-height layers that get confused with each other. The barometric formula reads an altitude straight off a pressure against a 1013.25 hPa standard sea level, and the hypsometric equation turns a known height change and a known temperature into the pressure you should expect to read there. Run them on the same numbers and the gap between them is the real weather.

    Two different layers, not two answers to the same question. The barometric
    formula 44330 (1 - (P/1013.25)^0.1903) inverts a standard atmosphere to read a
    height straight off a pressure. The hypsometric equation walks a known
    height difference from a known pressure with a fixed 6.5 K/km lapse rate, so it
    needs the temperature at the reference level. They agree at sea level and drift
    apart when the real air is not standard.
