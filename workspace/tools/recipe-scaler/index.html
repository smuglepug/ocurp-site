Recipe Scaler

Scale a recipe up or down by ingredient, keep the conversions sensible at any size, and swap between metric and US units.

Study & careerRecipeScaleCooking
Open now All tools

Scale a whole ingredient list by target over base servings using exact rational arithmetic, so a half cup doubles to one cup and a third of a cup triples to one cup rather than to 0.9999999. Converts metric and imperial where the conversion is real, and names the units where it is not.

EXACT FRACTIONS METRIC / IMPERIAL NO SILENT GUESSES

Every quantity is held as an exact fraction and multiplied as a fraction, so nothing is ever rounded into a lie. Doubling 1/2 cup gives 1 cup and tripling 1/3 cup gives 1 cup exactly — not 0.666667. Anything that genuinely will not convert is named in the row instead of being given a made-up number.
