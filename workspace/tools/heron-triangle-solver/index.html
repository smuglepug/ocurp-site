Heron's Triangle Solver

Area, perimeter, altitude, median and the inradius and circumradius of any triangle from its three sides, with an exact validity check on the triangle inequality before it computes anything.

Math & convertTriangleHeronGeometryArea
Open now All tools

Three side lengths in, everything Heron's formula and the law of cosines can tell you out: the semiperimeter, the area, all three angles in degrees, the altitude dropped to the longest side, the inradius and the circumradius. The triangle inequality is checked first, so lengths that cannot close — like 1, 2 and 5 — are refused by name, since Heron's radicand would otherwise go negative and every angle would come back as the arccosine of a number outside its domain. The angle sum is shown as it comes out, so you can see the three angles really do add to 180.
