text — CLIENT-SIDE

Render text with hand-drawn 3x3, 5x5 and 5x7 bitmap fonts, stacked, in a row, or sheared into an arc, with live ink-density stats. 3 FONTS3 LAYOUTSINK METRICSCLIENT-SIDE

How it works: each character is looked up in a hand-drawn bitmap font (3×3, 5×5 or 5×7) and the rows are concatenated. Anything not in the font renders as a question-mark block. Arc shears each letter by up to ±25% of its height, proportional to where it sits in the word.
