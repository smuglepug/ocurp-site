Voltage Divider Calculator

Vout, current and per-resistor power for a loaded or unloaded divider, plus the inverse solve for R1, with a warning as soon as the load impedance comes within 100x of R2 and the divider stops being ideal.

Notes

A divider is only ideal while the load draws nothing. The output impedance is R1∥R2, so once RL comes within about 100× of R2 the parallel pair is dominated by the load and Vout sags far below Vs·R2/(R1+R2). Buffer the output (op-amp follower) or use a much smaller divider impedance.
