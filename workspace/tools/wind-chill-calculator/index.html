Wind Chill & Heat Index

NWS wind chill at 50 F or below with 3 mph or more, clamped to the air temperature whenever the formula runs warm, and the Rothfusz heat index at 80 F or above with 40% RH or more. Outside both windows it says so.

The NWS wind chill and the Rothfusz heat index, with the validity windows enforced instead of ignored. Wind chill fires at 50 F or below with 3 mph or more and is clamped to the air temperature whenever the formula returns something warmer; heat index fires at 80 F or above with 40% RH or more; between those windows the answer is the air temperature.

Feels like
Working
Which branch fired, and why
NWS wind chill is valid only at 50 F or below with wind at 3 mph or more, and the official rule is to take whichever is warmer between the formula result and the air temperature. Heat index needs 80 F or above and 40% RH or more. In between the two windows the honest answer is the air temperature itself, so that is what this page reports.
