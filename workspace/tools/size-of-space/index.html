Size of Space

A scale ladder from 1 m to the observable universe: how many of a reference object fit.

A scale ladder from 1 metre to the observable universe: how many humans, cars or galaxies fit along each step, and how long it takes to travel it.

Where this step sits on the log axis
1 m10^6 m10^13 m10^20 m8.8e26 m
Scale step Length in metres Human-readable cars across

Ladder values are real measured astronomical distances. The observable universe is the radius of the light cone we can see; 8.8e26 m is the commonly quoted figure. A city is a plain 20 km reference, not a measured quantity.
