Readability Grade Level

Score any text for grade level with Flesch, Flesch-Kincaid and Gunning Fog side by side.

Writing & textReadabilityGradeText
Open now All tools

Score a passage on Flesch-Kincaid grade level and Flesch Reading Ease from a real syllable count, with a per-word breakdown of where the difficulty sits. FLESCH-KINCAIDREADING EASEPER-WORDCLIENT-SIDE

How it works: syllables are counted with a vowel-group heuristic plus a silent-final-e correction, then two independent formulas are applied: Flesch–Kincaid (0.39×words + 11.8×syllables − 15.59) and Flesch Reading Ease (206.835 − 1.015×words/sentence − 84.6×syllables/word). Syllables are integers, so the grade level can come out negative — that is reported as such, not clamped to zero.
