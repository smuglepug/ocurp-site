Wedding Budget Planner

Build a wedding budget by category, track what is paid, and see what is still owed.

Study & careerMoneyPlannerWedding
Open now All tools

Enter the total you have for the wedding, then type what you plan to spend and what you have already paid for each of twelve categories. The totals, the progress bars and the over-budget warning update as you type.

Currency is whatever you are using — figures are just numbers.

Suggested split — click to load

Auto-fill uses the classic planning guideline (venue 40%, catering 33%, photography 10%…). Rebalance rescales whatever you have typed, proportionally, so the planned column adds up to your total budget exactly.
