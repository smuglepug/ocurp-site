Rational Approximator

mathematics — CLIENT-SIDE

Continued fractions, convergents, and the tightest rational you can write with a denominator under your cap. Feed it an irrational and it hands back the ladder of fractions that bracket it ever more tightly - the reason 355 over 113 is still the best pi you can write on one line.

CONTINUED FRACTIONS CONVERGENTS BOUNDED DENOMINATOR

Every real number has a continued fraction: you subtract the whole part, invert the remainder, and repeat. The convergents that fall out are the best rational approximations there are - a theorem, not a guess, which is why 7/5 and 17/12 bracket sqrt 2 so tightly. The bounded search below then checks every denominator up to your cap by hand and reports the tightest fraction above and below, so the two halves of the tool can be compared against each other.
