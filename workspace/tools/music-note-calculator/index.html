Music Note Calculator

Work out frequency, note and octave from a pitch or a frequency, and hear the note.

Converts any note to its frequency in twelve-tone equal temperament from a reference pitch you set, and names any stray frequency to the nearest note with the deviation in cents. A4 is 440 Hz by definition; the octave above it is therefore exactly 880.

Twelve-tone equal temperament: one semitone is the twelfth root of two, so an octave is exactly a doubling.
