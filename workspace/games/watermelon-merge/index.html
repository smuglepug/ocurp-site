Pick a lane (or use the arrow keys) to drop the next fruit. Two fruits of the same rank that end up touching merge into the next rank up, and merges cascade. Grow a fruit of the target rank within the drop budget and you win; run out of drops before you make it, and you lose. The drop order is seeded and fixed — the level you read is the level you play.
