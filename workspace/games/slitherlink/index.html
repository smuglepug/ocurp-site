Slitherlink

Draw one closed loop

Size

Draw segments between the dots. A clue counts the segments on that cell's edges. You win when every dot has exactly two lines and every clue is satisfied. Click an edge to draw, right-click to cross it out.

Readout
How to play

Every dot must end with exactly two lines through it, so the lines close into loops. A 3 means three of that cell's four edges are lines. Clues may be blank, which means zero or more.
