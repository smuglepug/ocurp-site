THE EXACT RULES
Five orbit slots exist, numbered 1–5. Slot j sits at orbital period 90×j minutes and launch azimuth 30×(j−1) degrees. Each satellite carries a required period and a required launch azimuth; a slot only accepts a satellite whose requirements are within 45 minutes and 40 degrees of its own. Placing costs one move; pulling a satellite back costs one move. You have 9 moves, which is exactly the worst case: three wrong placements, three pulls, three correct placements. Three further rules must all hold at the end: no two satellites in adjacent slots (a debris corridor runs between neighbouring orbital shells) and the satellite needing the highest azimuth must hold the highest-numbered slot. WIN = all three satellites legally placed and every rule holding. FAIL = the move budget spent with a satellite still on the pad. Manifests are built from a known-good assignment and re-rolled unless a brute-force search finds exactly one assignment satisfying all of the above, so every manifest has a single answer.
