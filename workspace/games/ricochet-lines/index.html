Click a cell to place a mirror, click again to flip it, a third time to take it away.

The ball enters from the arrow and rolls in a straight line until something turns it. / turns it the other way, \ the other way again, and dark blocks stop it dead. Steer it into the ring. Every board here is generated by running the simulation backwards from the goal, so a solution always exists — the Show solution button plays it back.
