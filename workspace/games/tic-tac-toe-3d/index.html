YOUR MOVE PICK A CUBE

rotateX —20° · rotateY 20°

A 4×4×4 cube drawn with CSS 3D transforms — four stacked slices you drag or nudge. Fill four in a line to win, and there are 76 lines to do it on: 48 straight through the axes, 24 diagonals across the four slices of each plane, and the 4 long space diagonals of the cube. Sixty-four cubes, alternate turns, and the board that fills first along any of the 76 lines takes it.
