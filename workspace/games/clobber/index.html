Click one of your pieces, then an adjacent enemy piece to take it.

A move shifts one of your pieces one square up, down, left or right onto an enemy piece and removes it. A player with no move left loses. Every move removes exactly one piece, so the game always ends and can never be drawn. The three boards are small enough that an exhaustive search settles who wins each one before you play a single move - the page names the side to move's real advantage on the button you are about to press.
