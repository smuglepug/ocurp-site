YOUR MOVE - ANY BOARD PICK A SQUARE

Nine 3×3 boards in a 3×3 grid. Wherever you play, your opponent must answer in that same small board — that is the whole rule. You win by lining three up in a row on any one small board, or by taking three boards that lie in a line, column or diagonal across the big grid. The one escape hatch: if the board you are sent to is already full, you are free to play anywhere. Without that rule the game can lock, and a game that can lock is not a game.
