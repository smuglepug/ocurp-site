SPACE or CLICK = jump You run on your own - only the jump is yours

One button. The runner never stops, so the only question is when to jump. Every gap in the river is inside a single jump arc, and every stone is wide enough to land on after a slow reaction - the river gets wider as you climb, never impossible. Falling costs a life and puts you back at the near bank of the same level.
