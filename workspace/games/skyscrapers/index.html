THE EXACT RULES
Fill the n × n grid so that every row and every column holds 1–n exactly once. The numbers around the edges are clues: a clue counts how many buildings you see when you look along that line, and a building is seen only when it is taller than everything before it. A clue of n forces that line to start with the tallest building; a clue of 1 forces it to end with it. Grey squares are givens and cannot be changed. A number that repeats in its row or column is REFUSED and costs nothing; a legal number that disagrees with the answer is kept, costs one mistake and can be overwritten. WIN = every square filled, every row and column a permutation, and all 4n clues matched. Puzzles are cut from a freshly generated full grid and are only handed over when the solver counts exactly one solution.
