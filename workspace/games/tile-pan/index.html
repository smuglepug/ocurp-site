CLICK A PIECE THEN A CELL

Every shape below was cut from one 6×5 sheet, so the pieces always tile the pan exactly. Pick a piece, spin or mirror it, then click a cell to drop its top-left square there. Nothing overlaps and nothing hangs off the edge. Fill all thirty cells to win.

Ghosted cells show where the held piece would land; red means it will not fit.
