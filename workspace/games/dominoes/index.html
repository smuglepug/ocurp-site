Your hand

A full double-six set of 28 tiles. Play a tile that matches either open end of the chain. Six tiles each, sixteen in the boneyard. Play a tile and the chain grows; no tile that fits means you draw until one does (or pass once the boneyard is empty). The round winner scores the pips left in the opponent’s hand: an empty hand wins outright, and if both sides are stuck the lighter hand wins the difference. First to the target score takes the match. The AI is one-ply greedy: it takes the heaviest tile it can and prefers to break its own doubles last.
