Ludo

Roll, race four tokens round the board and capture your rivals on the way home.

Board & cards
Play now All games
—
PLAYER 1 TO MOVE ROLL THE DIE

Four tokens each, on a 52-cell ring around a 15×15 cross board. You need a 6 to bring a token out of the yard onto your start square; from there it walks the ring and then a 5-square home column. Land on a lone opponent token and it goes back to the yard — two or more tokens on a square make it safe. A token needs the exact number to stop in the home column, and all four tokens home wins the game. A 6 earns another roll, up to three 6s in a row. If a roll has no legal move the turn simply passes — the game can never deadlock. Player 1 is you.
