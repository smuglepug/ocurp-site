Every puzzle is built by clicking random cells away from the all-off board, so one is guaranteed to exist.

Clicking a cell toggles it and its four orthogonal neighbours. Clear every light to win. The Auto-solve button replays the move list the puzzle was generated from, so you can see a real solution rather than a claim that one exists.
