THE EXACT RULES
Three branch resistors R1, R2, R3 each take an integer 1–6, and no two of them may read the same value. Every displayed Kirchhoff equation has to hold at the same time. Turning a dial to a different value costs one adjustment, and the budget is 8 / 7 / 6 adjustments on easy, medium, hard — a solved network needs exactly 3, so you get at least twice the necessary allowance. WIN = all equations read true. FAIL = the budget is spent with an equation still false. A value that repeats one you already used is refused and costs nothing. Networks are built from a hidden working assignment and are re-rolled unless a brute-force search over all 120 legal assignments finds exactly one that satisfies every equation, so every network you are shown has a single answer.
