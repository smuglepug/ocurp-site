Grid Trail

Leave a light trail, cut off three hunters and survive for sixty seconds.

Puzzle
Play now All games
SCORE 0 LIVES 3 RIDERS 3 TIME 60.0 HOLD THE LINE

Tap the board to turn right, tap again to turn left — or hold ← → / A D. Your light trail is solid: cross your own line and it is a self-crash. Three riders hunt you down, but they cannot cross your trail either — cut one off for 250 points. You start at 170 px/s and gain 1 px/s per second to a 300 px/s ceiling. Survive the full 60 s to hold the arena. 3 lives.
