How this works

1 · Encode

A small convolutional encoder maps a frame to a latent vector. Two nearby frames produce nearby latents — that smoothness is the whole inductive bias.

2 · Predict in latent space

A predictor maps (context latent, action) toward the target latent. The loss is squared error between representations. No pixel loss is ever computed.

3 · Roll out

Feeding the prediction back in as the next context gives a multi-step forecast. That is the property pixels-reconstructing models do not have.
