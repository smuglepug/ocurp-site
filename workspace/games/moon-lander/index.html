Moon Lander

Burn, rotate and touch down softly on the landing pad.

Arcade
Play now All games
FUEL 100% ALT 0 0 / 0 BEST 0 READY

↑ or Space to burn, ← → to rotate. Touch: hold THRUST, tap to turn. You start drifting toward the pad and have 30 px/s of it for free -- you need roughly 4 seconds of descent to arrive. Land on the pad below 42 px/s down and 26 px/s sideways or you crack it.
