Lamplight

Place bulbs to light every cell, without any two bulbs seeing each other.

Click an empty cell to place a bulb. Click a bulb to take it back.

A bulb lights its whole row and column until a wall. Two bulbs may never see each other along a clear line, and every open cell must end up lit. Dark cells are unlit; orange cells are lit. Every puzzle is generated by lighting one arrangement and then blanking bulbs off it, and is kept only when an exhaustive check finds exactly one solution - so each board has one right answer, never zero and never two.
