Click a mirror to flip it. The beam leaves the orange port on the grid edge, turns wherever it meets a mirror, and stops at a blocker. Land it on the target ring to clear the level.

Loading…

THE EXACT RULES
Rotate mirrors, do not move anything. WIN = the beam reaches the target ring. A level you cannot read within its move budget resets itself, with its mirrors back where they started, and each reset costs one of your three lives on that level. FAIL = a fourth reset — the run restarts from level 1. Each level's budget is three moves per mirror plus six, and every level was built from a solution that a brute-force search proved is the only solution on the board.
