▲ ladder climbs · ▼ snake slides Land exactly on 100 to win · a 6 rolls again (max 3 in a row)

The classic race up 100 squares with 8 ladders and 8 snakes. Roll, count forward, and if you stop on a ladder foot or a snake head you jump immediately — one jump per roll, never a chain. Overshooting 100 is not allowed: you bounce back and wait for the exact roll. A 6 earns another turn, up to three in a row, so no player can stall the table. First token onto 100 wins outright.
