Left-click reveals, right-click flags. The first click is always safe — the charges are laid after you click.

A hex touches up to six neighbours, so a number can read 0 to 6. Every field is generated lazily: the first click is cleared and its neighbours re-counted, so you can never open on a charge. Clear all safe hexes to win; open a charge and the field is lost.
