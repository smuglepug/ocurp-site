Lumen Grid

Light every white cell with bulbs that never see each other. One proven solution.

ONE SOLUTION — PROVEN

Click a white cell to place or lift a bulb. Every white cell must be lit, and no two bulbs may see each other. Two bulbs that cross on different rows and columns can light the same cell - that is fine. The number in a black cell counts the bulbs touching it.
