Every maze is carved by walking a single path across the grid and knocking through the wall it steps through, so the open passages form a tree: the number you have to walk is fixed by the maze, and there is a route to the exit in every single one. Reach the orange gate. Time is the only score.

Orange cell is the exit, dimmed orange is the route the solver found. Click a cell to jump there only when it is adjacent - walls still stop you, exactly as they stop the keyboard.
