Bomberman Lite

Bomb through crates and corner the enemies without catching your own blast.

Arcade
Play now All games
SCORE 0 LIVES 5 CRATES 36 FOES 4 READY

Move with WASD or the arrow keys, drop a bomb with Space, restart with R. Crates are destructible, blast wraps a 3-cell cross, and it stops at walls. Enemies walk one square every half second straight at you. Bait a bomb next to an enemy, then walk out of the cross — your own blast kills you too. Clear all 4 to win.
