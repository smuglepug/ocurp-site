Ricochet Rings

Flick a disc off the walls to take down every ring target. Three levels.

Arcade
Play now All games
LEVEL 1 TARGETS 3 LIVES 3 SCORE 0 AIM AND FLICK

Press on the board, drag away from the disc, release to fire — it slingshots opposite your pull. Grey rings are solid: one touch costs a life and returns the disc to its pad, but targets you already downed stay down. Orange rings are targets. Clear every target to advance; clear all 3 levels to win. A disc in flight for 10s is also a lost life, so bank shots beat lazy ones.
