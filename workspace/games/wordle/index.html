THE EXACT RULES
6 rows. Type any 5-letter word from this page's own list of WORDS five-letter words — anything else is rejected and does not use a row. Feedback: green = right letter, right place; amber = in the word, wrong place; grey = not in the word. A letter you guess twice is marked amber at most as many times as it really occurs in the answer. HARD MODE: a green letter must be reused in that exact square, and a letter already marked amber may not be reused at all once the answer's copy of it has been found. A guess breaking those rules is rejected and costs no row. WIN = the answer inside 6 rows. FAIL = 6 rows used, answer still unguessed. Hard mode cannot lock you out: the answer word itself is always a legal guess, since it reuses every green in place and never re-places a spent amber.
