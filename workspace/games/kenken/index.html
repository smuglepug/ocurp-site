CLICK A CELL THEN TYPE 1-6

Every row and every column holds 1 to n once each. The little label in each cage is the arithmetic its cells must satisfy: 12+ sums to twelve, 3× multiplies to three, 7− differ by seven and 4÷ divides evenly into four. A cage of one cell is just that cell's own number.

Click a cell to select it, then type a digit. Backspace clears it, arrow keys move. Every puzzle is checked for a single solution before it reaches you.
