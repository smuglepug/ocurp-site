Ederdrone

An open, five-inch autonomous quadcopter. I designed the carbon-fibre frame in CAD and I'm sharing the design for free, so anyone can build one.

Design stage: not built or flown yet
Get the CAD design Follow the build
The design

Small, stiff, and built to fly itself.

A five-inch class frame: big enough to carry a companion computer and camera, small enough to stay cheap to build and repair.

  • Class5-inch quad
  • FrameCarbon fibre
  • Flight stackPX4
  • AutonomyROS 2
